= Learn about verified organizations in NetApp Console
:hardbreaks:
:nofooter:
:icons: font
:linkattrs:
:imagesdir: ./media/

[.lead]
A verified organization links your organization in the NetApp Console to your NetApp business account identity (for example, a CMAT ID). This helps the Console apply a consistent organization identity and governance model across NetApp systems.

== Why verify your organization
Verifying your organization can help you:

* Align your Console organization with your NetApp business account identity.
* Improve consistency for administration, reporting, and support workflows.
* Reduce ambiguity when your teams work across multiple NetApp services.
* Support governance controls that depend on a trusted organization identity.

== What changes after verification
After your organization is verified:

* The organization is associated with a NetApp business account identity.
* The Console can treat that organization identity as authoritative for business-level mapping.
* Some organization profile changes might be restricted in the Console if they are managed by the source system.

== Before you verify
Before you verify your organization, confirm the following:

* You have the required administrative role for your organization.
* You have the organization details needed for verification.
* You have coordinated with your NetApp account team if business-account mapping needs validation.
