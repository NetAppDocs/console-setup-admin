//tag::intro-steps[]
.Steps

. Select *Administration > Identity and access*. 
. Select *Federation* to view the *Federations* page.

. Select *Configure new federation*.

//end::intro-steps[]



//tag::federation-test-steps[]
. Select *Next*. 

. Select *Test connection* to test your connection. You are directed to a login page for your IdP server. Log in with your IdP credentials. After you log in, go back to the Console to enable the connection.

+
NOTE: When using the Console in restricted mode, copy the URL to either an incognito browser window or a separate browser to log in to your IdP.

. In the Console, select *Next* to review the summary page.


. Set up notifications. 
+
Choose between seven days and 30 days. The system emails expiry notifications and shows them in the Console to any user with the following roles: Super admin, Org admin, Federation admin, and Federation viewer.

. Review the federation details and then select *Enable federation*.

. Select *Finish* to complete the process.

After you enable the federation, users log in to the NetApp Console using their corporate credentials.
//end::federation-test-steps[]



//tag::federation-verify-domain[]
. Enter your domain details:
.. Choose whether you want to use a verified domain or your email domain. The email domain is the domain associated with the account you are logged in with.
.. Enter the name of the federation you are configuring.
.. If you choose a verified domain, select the domain from the list.    

. Select *Next*.
//end::federation-verify-domain[]



//tag::federation-prereq[]
.Before you begin
* An IdP account with administrative privileges is required. Coordinate with your IdP administrator to complete the steps.
* Identify the domain you want to use for federation. You can use your email domain or a different domain that you own. If you want to use a domain other than your email domain, you must first verify the domain in the Console. For instructions, see link:task-federation-verify-domain.html[Verify your domain in NetApp Console].
//end::federation-prereq[]
