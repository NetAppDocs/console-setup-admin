= Verify your NetApp Console organization
:hardbreaks:
:nofooter:
:icons: font
:linkattrs:
:imagesdir: ./media/

[.lead]
By default, Digital Advisor shows all NetApp accounts associated with your company, rather than just your Console organization. To limit what is shown in Digital Advisor to a specific account, verify the business account you want to use with your Console organization.


== Before you begin
You must have the Organization admin role to verify your organization. Your user must also be associated with a NetApp Support Site (NSS) account. link:task-adding-nss-accounts.html[Learn how to associate your NSS account].

NOTE: If your company has only one business account and your user is associated with a NetApp Support Site account, the Console automatically verifies your organization.


== Verify your organization



.Steps

. If your user has access to more than one organization, select the organization you want to verify from the Organization drop-down menu.

. In *Administration >  Identity and access*.

. Select *Organization*.

. Next to the page title, select *Verify*.
+
image:screenshot-iam-verify-org.png[A screenshot of the Organization page with the Verify button highlighted.]
+
NOTE: If your company has only one business account, the tag next to the page header will say *Verified* and there is no action to take. 

. In the *Associate business account* dialog, select the business account you want to associate with your Console organization from the drop-down menu. Then select *Associate*.
