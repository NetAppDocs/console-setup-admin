= Verify your organization in NetApp Console
:hardbreaks:
:nofooter:
:icons: font
:linkattrs:
:imagesdir: ./media/

[.lead]
Verifying your organization links your Console organization with your NetApp business account identity. This allows you to use features such as the Digital Advisor and AIQ recommendations.

.Required roles
Org admin or Super admin. link:reference-iam-predefined-roles.html[Learn more about access roles.]

If your company has more than one business account with NetApp, you must verify your organization and associate it with the correct business account. If your company has only one business account with NetApp, your organization is automatically verified and linked to that business account.

When your Console organization is associated with your business account, Digital Advisor shows you only resources associated with that account. If you have multiple business accounts, you'll need to select one to associate with your Console organization. If you need to change the association, you can verify your organization again and select a different business account.


.Steps

. Select *Administration > Identity and access*.
. Select *Organization*.
. On the *Organization* page, select *Verify* next to the page title.
+
image:screenshot-iam-verify-org.png[A screenshot of the Organization page that shows the Verify button next to the page title.]
. From the *Associate business account* dialog, choose the business account to associate with your organization.
