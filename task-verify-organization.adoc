= Verify your organization in NetApp Console
:hardbreaks:
:nofooter:
:icons: font
:linkattrs:
:imagesdir: ./media/

[.lead]
Verify your organization to link the organization in the NetApp Console with your NetApp business account identity.

.Required roles
You need an organization-level administrative role to verify an organization. link:reference-iam-predefined-roles.html[Learn more about access roles.]

.Before you begin
* Gather the organization details required for the verification flow.
* Confirm that your organization is not already verified.

.Steps

. Select *Administration > Identity and access*.
. Select *Organization*.
. On the *Organization* page, start the verification workflow.
. Enter the required organization details and continue.
. Review the organization match and confirm verification.
. Verify that the organization status shows as verified.

.Result
Your Console organization is linked to your NetApp business account identity.

.If verification does not complete
* Confirm that the organization details are correct.
* Confirm that the organization is eligible for verification.
* If needed, contact NetApp Support and include your organization ID. link:task-get-help.html[Get help].
